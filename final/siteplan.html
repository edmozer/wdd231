BYU-I Student Portfolio Site Plan

Site Name

BYU-I Student Portfolio
This name was chosen to represent a personal portfolio for BYU-Idaho students, showcasing projects, skills, and achievements.

Site Purpose

The site provides a platform for students to display their work, share contact information, and connect with potential employers or collaborators.

Scenarios

  • How can I view the student's latest web development projects?
  • Where can I find contact information for networking or job opportunities?

Color Scheme

  • Primary Blue (#003366): Used for headings, navigation bar, and buttons.
  • Accent Gold (#ffd700): Used for highlights, icons, and call-to-action elements.
  • Background Light (#f6f7f8): Used for page backgrounds and cards.

Typography

  • Inter (Headings, navigation, buttons)
  • Arial (Body text, paragraphs)
